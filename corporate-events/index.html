Corporate coffee cart questions

What kinds of company events do you serve?

Office gatherings, company celebrations, launches, openings, meetings, client events and brand activations can all be planned around the cart.

Can the presentation match our event or brand?

Yes. Menus, signs, cart styling and botanical details can be planned around the event and desired presentation.

What drinks can be served?

Menus can include espresso drinks, matcha, chai, tea and hot chocolate, along with syrups and alternative milk options. The final menu is confirmed with the quote.

Do you travel outside Los Angeles?

We serve Los Angeles and surrounding areas. Additional travel charges may apply depending on the event location.

How do we receive a quote?

Share the date, venue or city, estimated guest count and any ideas for the menu or presentation. We’ll use those details to prepare a personalized quote.
