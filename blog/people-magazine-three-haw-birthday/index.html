The Garden Coffee Co. Featured in PEOPLE Magazine

Coffee from The Garden Coffee Co. was part of a Los Angeles “Three-Haw” celebration covered by PEOPLE.

September 8, 20264-minute readBy The Garden Coffee Co.

PEOPLE’s coverage of Barbie Blank Coba’s twins’ third birthday named The Garden Coffee Co. among the Los Angeles event’s food and drink vendors.

What the coverage confirms: PEOPLE reported that, while the children enjoyed birthday treats, adults had coffee from The Garden Coffee Co. The publication’s photographs and article are not reproduced here.

A coffee moment inside the celebration

The event used a “Three-Haw” Western theme for twins Brooklyn and Jaxen’s third birthday. The coffee service gave adult guests their own refreshment moment alongside the celebration planned for the children.

Read the original PEOPLE feature for the publication’s complete reporting and photography.

Why a coffee cart works at a birthday event

A mobile espresso bar can serve a different role from dessert or a children’s refreshment station. Guests can order a made-to-order coffee or matcha, gather near the cart and return to the rest of the event without leaving the venue.

For daytime and family celebrations, a focused menu can include espresso drinks, matcha and non-coffee options. The final selection should be planned around the event schedule, guest count and venue.

Plan the service around the gathering

Useful details to share when requesting a birthday or private-event quote include:

  • The date, venue or city and estimated guest count
  • When guests arrive and when coffee should begin
  • Whether the event has a theme, color direction or custom sign
  • Which espresso, matcha and alternative drink options matter most
  • Any known load-in, outdoor or venue-access considerations

Those details make it possible to recommend a service plan that fits the room and the people in it. For more planning help, read our Los Angeles coffee cart catering guide or browse the real event gallery.
