# The Garden Coffee Co. — SEO audit

Audit date: September 7, 2026
Primary market: Los Angeles
Primary goal: qualified inquiries for weddings, corporate events and private events

## Executive summary

The homepage already communicated the brand well, but the site had only one indexable page. It could not separately answer the searches people make when they compare a Los Angeles coffee cart, plan a wedding or evaluate pricing. This first SEO pass creates that missing search architecture while preserving the current visual identity.

The largest remaining growth opportunities are not more keywords in the code. They are Google Search Console setup, a complete Google Business Profile, genuine reviews and venue/vendor links, and dedicated service pages supported by real event photos and details.

## Completed in this pass

- Rewrote the homepage title description to clearly describe Los Angeles coffee cart catering, weddings, corporate events and private parties.
- Removed the obsolete meta-keywords tag.
- Added canonical URLs and social-sharing metadata to the new pages.
- Expanded homepage structured data into a WebSite and LocalBusiness graph with a service-area offer catalog.
- Added a crawlable Planning Guides section to the homepage and Blog links in the main navigation, mobile navigation and footer.
- Created a blog hub and three substantive Los Angeles guides:
  - Coffee Cart Catering in Los Angeles: What to Know Before You Book
  - How to Plan a Coffee Cart for Your Los Angeles Wedding
  - How Much Does Coffee Cart Catering Cost in Los Angeles?
- Added BlogPosting and breadcrumb structured data to every article.
- Added contextual internal links among the guides and to the quote form.
- Updated the XML sitemap with every new canonical URL and accurate modification dates.

## Priority actions after launch

### 1. Measurement and indexing

- Verify the domain in Google Search Console.
- Submit `https://thegardencoffeecart.com/sitemap.xml`.
- Request indexing for the homepage, blog hub and three launch articles.
- Install GA4 or another privacy-conscious analytics setup and record quote-form submissions as conversions.
- Connect ad-platform tracking only after consent and privacy requirements are addressed.

### 2. Trust and local relevance

- Replace any sample or unverified testimonial with a genuine customer quote, name and permission to publish.
- Complete or improve the Google Business Profile as a service-area business.
- Ask paid wedding and yacht clients for Google reviews that naturally mention the event type and city.
- Build venue-planner relationships and earn links from preferred-vendor pages, wedding blogs and local event directories.
- Add real event case studies with event type, neighborhood or venue when permitted, guest count, menu and photos.

### 3. Conversion quality

- Show the starting package price clearly and explain what is included.
- Add a short venue-requirements block: power, water, access, service footprint and travel area.
- Add real operating details to the quote form: date, location, guest count, event type and desired service duration.
- Add a visible phone number only if the business can reliably answer or return calls.

## Content cadence

Publish one strong, original page every two to four weeks. Each post should answer a real booking question, include first-hand Garden Coffee experience and link to one relevant service or quote page. Useful next topics:

- How many coffee drinks should you plan per wedding guest?
- Best times to serve coffee at a Los Angeles wedding
- Espresso, matcha or both: building an event menu
- What Los Angeles venues need to know before a coffee cart arrives
- Corporate coffee cart ideas for office events and brand activations

Avoid producing multiple thin pages that differ only by a city or keyword. First-hand photos, event details and honest answers will be more defensible and more useful.

## Known limitations

- No Google Search Console, Google Business Profile or analytics account was available during this code audit, so impressions, rankings, conversions and index coverage could not be verified.
- The PageSpeed Insights API did not return a usable report during the audit. A live Lighthouse/PageSpeed test should be run after deployment.
- Rankings cannot be guaranteed. Search visibility will depend on indexing, site quality, competition, reviews, links, engagement and continued publication.
