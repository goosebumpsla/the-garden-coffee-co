# Local SEO authority plan

Prepared September 8, 2026 for The Garden Coffee Co. This plan uses only genuine customer, venue, vendor, and event activity. It does not recommend fabricated reviews, purchased links, fake locations, or thin city pages.

## Positioning

- Keep the current broad service areas because the business accepts distant events with an additional travel charge.
- Treat Los Angeles as the primary relevance center in website copy and structured data. Do not imply storefronts or offices in cities where none exist.
- Explain travel fees naturally on pricing and inquiry pages rather than duplicating the same page for every city.

Google says local results are mainly based on relevance, distance, and prominence. Complete profile information supports relevance; links and reviews contribute to prominence. A large service-area list does not remove the distance factor. [Google: improve local ranking](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091)

## Highest-priority recurring work

1. After each real completed event, send that customer the Business Profile review link once. Do not offer discounts, gifts, or other incentives for a review. Reply personally to every genuine review, including neutral or negative feedback.
2. Add a small set of fresh, truthful event photos to the Business Profile after real events. Use images that show the cart, drinks, styling, and service environment; never upload stock images as customer work.
3. Ask venues and vendors involved in a real event whether they maintain a preferred-vendor, recap, or partner page. Suggest a factual link to the most relevant Garden page only when the relationship is real and the page helps their audience.
4. Publish one useful article when there is first-hand information to contribute. Favor real questions from inquiries: venue power and water, service timing, guest-count planning, hot-weather menus, customization lead times, and travel-fee expectations.
5. Add original photos, a named author or reviewer, and concrete experience to each article when available. Google recommends clear authorship and people-first content that demonstrates first-hand expertise. [Google: helpful, reliable, people-first content](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content)

## 90-day cadence

### Every completed event

- Ask once for an honest Google review using the official review link or QR code.
- Save approved, rights-cleared photos and the factual event details that could support a gallery caption or future article.
- Record venue and vendor names only with permission before publishing or requesting a link.

Google explicitly prohibits incentives for reviews and recommends requesting honest reviews through its link or QR code. [Google: tips to get more reviews](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3474122)

### Weekly

- Check pending Business Profile edits and respond to any new reviews.
- Confirm the website, phone, service link, social profile, hours, and special hours remain accurate.
- Review Search Console for indexing changes, manual actions, or a sustained rise in crawl errors; do not repeatedly request indexing for unchanged URLs.

### Monthly

- Publish or substantially improve one first-hand planning resource if there is genuinely useful material.
- Add selected real-event images to the gallery and Business Profile.
- Review Search Console clicks and queries, Business Profile calls/website clicks, and accepted inquiries. Use these to improve useful pages, not to mass-produce keyword variants.
- Check that venue/vendor mentions and backlinks are accurate and still live.

## Practical content backlog

Only publish an item when the business can add original experience, accurate details, and appropriate images.

- Coffee cart power and water requirements for Los Angeles venues
- How many service hours a guest count actually needs
- Espresso, matcha, and non-coffee menu planning for mixed guest groups
- Outdoor summer event setup and drink-menu considerations
- What custom cups, signage, and cart styling require in lead time
- How travel charges work for events outside the core Los Angeles area
- A real event recap with venue/vendor attribution and permission

## Measurement

- Baseline: Search Console currently reports 10 total web-search clicks, 1 indexed page, and 3 redirect variants excluded; the indexing report was last updated September 3, 2026. Six new URLs were submitted afterward and are still processing.
- Track monthly: indexed canonical URLs, non-brand search queries, organic clicks, Business Profile actions, real review count and rating, referring venue/vendor domains, and accepted quote inquiries.
- Evaluate trends over at least 8–12 weeks. Google notes that improvements can take days to months and do not guarantee a ranking change. [Google: core update timing](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/core-updates)

## Guardrails

- No purchased, exchanged, or mass-directory backlinks.
- No fake reviews, review gating, or review incentives.
- No virtual-office or fake-address listings.
- No city pages without distinct, useful, first-hand information.
- No claims about customers, venues, awards, or service history without evidence.
- No outreach or public replies without explicit approval from the business owner.
