Website privacy notice

Updated September 8, 2026. This notice describes the inquiry, scheduling and optional advertising features on thegardencoffeecart.com, operated for The Garden Coffee Co.

Information you choose to send

When you request a quote, we receive the name, email, phone number and event details you provide. Some fields are optional. We use inquiry information to respond, discuss availability and prepare a proposal. FormSubmit processes the form and delivers it to our contact email.

The form also includes the page address and campaign labels, when present, so we can understand which page or campaign brought an inquiry. Please do not include payment-card details or other sensitive information in your message.

Optional planning calls

You can request a quote without scheduling a call. After a successful inquiry, a Google Calendar booking page is embedded on the website. Using it connects your browser to Google; Google handles the scheduling details and Google Meet invitation under its own privacy policy. A calendar being displayed does not mean a call has been booked.

Optional advertising measurement

If you allow advertising tracking, we use Meta Pixel and Meta Conversions API to understand website visits, inquiry activity and advertising performance. Meta can receive page and event information, browser and network information, and advertising-cookie identifiers. For accepted inquiries, the server can also send hashed email and phone details to help Meta match events. Hashing does not make this information anonymous.

We do not start optional Meta tracking until you allow it through the website controls. Declining it does not prevent you from browsing, requesting a quote or scheduling a call. A browser Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track signal keeps this optional advertising tracking off.

Your tracking choices

We store your choice in this browser's local storage. It applies to this browser, not automatically to your other devices. You can change it below or through the footer. If you turn tracking off, it stops future advertising events from this site and clears the Meta cookies this site controls; this does not erase information previously sent to Meta.

Choose whether to allow optional advertising tracking.

Services used by this website

Cloudflare and Netlify provide website and server-function hosting during our hosting transition. FormSubmit handles inquiry delivery. Google supplies website fonts and, when opened, the booking calendar. Meta receives optional advertising data only when allowed. These providers may process information needed to deliver their services, including network or browser information.

Questions and requests

To ask about information associated with your inquiry, retention, correction or deletion, email contact.thegardenco@gmail.com. Tell us which inquiry your request concerns, without sending sensitive identity documents by email. Information held directly by another provider is also subject to that provider's own policies and request process.

Changes to this notice

Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. This notice covers this website; it does not describe unrelated services or external websites you may visit.
