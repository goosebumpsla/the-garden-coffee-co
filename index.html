Before you inquire

Coffee cart catering questions

Helpful details for planning a mobile espresso bar in Los Angeles and surrounding areas.

What areas do you serve?

We serve Los Angeles and surrounding areas. Travel outside our core service area may carry an additional charge, based on the event location.

How is each event quoted?

Quotes are personalized around the event date, location, guest count, service needs, menu and custom details. Share what you know and we’ll recommend an appropriate setup.

What can be included on the menu?

Our event menus can include espresso drinks, matcha, chai, tea and hot chocolate, along with syrups and alternative milk options. Final selections are confirmed with your quote.

Can the coffee cart be customized for the event?

Yes. Menus, signs, cart styling and botanical details can be planned to complement weddings, company gatherings and private celebrations.

Can you serve outdoors?

Outdoor service is possible. Venue access, weather and safe operating conditions are reviewed while planning the setup.

What venue information should I share?

Include the venue or city, estimated guest count and any known load-in or setup details. We’ll confirm the practical requirements before the event.
